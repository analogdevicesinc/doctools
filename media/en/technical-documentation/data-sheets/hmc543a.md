## General Description

The  HMC543ALC4B  is  a  4-bit  digital  phase  shifter which is rated from 8 to 12 GHz, providing 0 to 360 degrees  of  phase  coverage,  with  a  LSB  of  22.5 degrees. The HMC543ALC4B  features  very low RMS  phase  error  of  4  degrees  and  extremely  low insertion  loss  variation  of  ±0.8  dB  across  all  phase states.  This high accuracy phase shifter is controlled with  complementary  logic  of  0/-3V,  and  requires  no fixed bias voltage.  The HMC543ALC4B is housed in a  compact  4x4  mm  ceramic  leadless  SMT  package and is internally matched to 50 Ohms with no external components.  Simple  external  level  shifting  circuitry can  be  used  to  convert  a  positive  CMOS  control voltage into complementary negative control signals.
