## Voltage Reference

The voltage at REFOUT is 5V ± 10mV at +25°C. The reference is internally connected to the DAC and is bufferedtoaccommodatetheDAC'svariableimpedance. This buffer is capable of driving the DAC, the RoFS resistor, and up to 500uA of external current. MAx507/ MAx508specificationsaredeterminedwiththeinternal reference.Thereference should bedecoupled at REFOUT with 10Q in series with the recommended decoupling capacitors, 10μF in parallel with 0.1μF.
