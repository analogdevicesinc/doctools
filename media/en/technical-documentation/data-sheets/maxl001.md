## Goneral Description

TheMAXLo01is a 100uH inductordesignedas themain magnetic component for simple flybackDC-DC converters with up to 3W output.Quieter than bobbin inductors and less costly than MolypermalloyPowder(MPP)or pot-core inductors,theMAXLo01isideal for distributed power supplies and portable equipment. The MAXL001 corematerial isan iron-powdercompound selectedfor 200kHz.The MAXL001hasradial leadsforPrinted-Circuit (PC) board mounting.
