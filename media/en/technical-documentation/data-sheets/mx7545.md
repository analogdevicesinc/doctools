## CMoS 12-Bit Buffered Multiplying DAC

## General Description

The MX7545 is a 12-bit CMOS multiplying digital-toanalogconverter(DAC）withinternaldatalatches. Inputdataisloadedasa\_single12-bitwordand latchedunderthecontrolofCSandWRinputs.When CSandWRarelow,theinputdatalatchesaretransparent and theDACoutput responds to any changes in the digital input.

Electricalcharacteristicsarespecifiedatbothofthese supply voltages.With a +5V supply,the digital inputs are+5VTTLandCMOScompatiblewhilehighvoltage CMOS compatibility is maintained at+15V supply range.

MaximMX7545useslowtempcothin-filmresistors thataretypically±%LSBandgainerrorsofmaximum whicharelaser-trimmedtoresultinlinearityerrors ±1 LSB (G grade).

Thedigitalinputsaredesignedwithimprovedprotection against electrostatic discharge(ESD) damage andcantypicallywithstandtoover6,0oovofESD voltages.

TheMX7545issuppliedin20-leadnarrowDIPand SmallOutlinepackages.
