## General Description

TheMaximMxL1007operationalamplifierfeatureslownoise, ±15V performance: 2.5nV/Hz wideband noise, 1/f cornerfrequency of 2Hz,and 60nVp-p 0.1Hz to 10Hz noise.Precision and speed performance includes 10uV typicaloffsetvoltage,0.2uV/Cdrift,130dBCMRRand PSRR, and an 8MHz unity-gain stable bandwidth. In addition, the MXL1007's voltage gain is 20 million with a 2kΩ2 loadand12millionwitha600Qload.

Maxim'sMXL1007is apin-compatiblealternativetoother industry-standardlow-noise opamps suchas theOP27 and LT1007.

For applications requiring higher performance, see the MAX427/MAX437andMAX410/MAX412/MAX414data sheets.
