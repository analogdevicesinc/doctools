Included in the HMC-DK005 Designer's Kit

## General Description

The  HMC536MS8G  &amp;  HMC536MS8GE  are  DC  to 6 GHz GaAs MMIC T/R switches in 8 lead MSOP8G surface  mount  packages  with  an  exposed  ground paddle. The switch is ideal for cellular/PCS/3G basestation  applications  featuring  low  0.5  dB  insertion  loss  and  +55  dBm input IP3. Power handling is excellent  up  through  6  GHz  with  the  switch  offering a P0.1dB compression point of +29 dBm at +3 volts control. On-chip circuitry allows positive voltage control of 0/+3 volts or 0/+5 volts at very low DC currents.
