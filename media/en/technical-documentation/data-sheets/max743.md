TheMAx743DC-DCconverterICcontainsalltheactive circuitryneededtobuildsmall,dual-outputpowersupplies.Relyingonsimpletwo-terminalinductorsrather thantransformers,theMAx743regulatesbothoutputs independentlytowithin±4%overallconditionsofline voltage,temperature,andloadcurrent.

## Operating Principle

Eachcurrent-modecontrollerconsistsofasumming amplifierthataddsthreesignals:thecurrentwaveform fromthepowerswitchFET,aVOUT-VREFerrorsignal, andarampsignalforACcompensationgeneratedbythe oscillator(Figure 3).The outputof the summing amplifier gates a flip-flop,which in turn drives the power FET switch.

Bothswitches aresynchronized totheoscillatorand turn onsimultaneouslywhentheflip-flopsareset.Thetransistorsturnoffindividuallywhentheirswitchcurrentsreach atripthresholddeterminedbytheVOUT-VREFerror signal.Thiscreatesaduty-cyclemodulatedpulsetrain at20okHz,wheretheontimeisproportionaltoboththe outputvoltageerrorsignalandthepeakinductorcurrent. Lowpeakcurrentsorhighoutputvoltageerrorsignals result in a high duty cycle (up to 90% maximum).

TheMAx743oscillatorfrequencyisgeneratedwithout external componentsandisnotadjustable.Thedevice is laser-trimmedto20okHzatwaferlevel,resultinginclean, predictable operation.
