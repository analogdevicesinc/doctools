## Power Dissipation

Power dissipation of the MAx626/7/8 consists of:

- 1) Input inverter losses;
- 3) Output current (either capacitive or resistive).
- 2) Crowbar current through the output devices;

The sum of these must be kept below the maximum power dissipation limit.

The DC input inverter losses are 0.4mA when both inputs are low and 4mA when both inputs are high.

The Crowbar current through an output device makingatransitionisapproximately10omAforafew nanoseconds. This is a smallportion of the total supply current, except for high switching frequencies or a small load capacitance (10opF).
