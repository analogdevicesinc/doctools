## High Speed Quad SPST Analog Switch

## General Description

TheMAx334isaquad\_single-pole-single-throw, patiblewiththeHarrisHl-201HSandSiliconixDG271. TheMAx334hasguaranteedbreak-before-make switching (torr &lt; ton), while featuring fast switching speeds.Turn-ontime is less than 100ns and turn-off time isless than50ns;channelonresistance is50 ohms maximum.CMOS inputs provide reduced input loadingandverylowleakagecurrents.

TheMAx334isalsoadirectreplacementforthe DG201 and DG211, featuring % the on resistance and five times the speed.

The MAx334 may be used with split supplies (±5V to ±15V)or singlepositive supplies(+5Vto+30V)while retaining CMOS and TTL iogic compatible inputs, and maintaining high switching speed.
