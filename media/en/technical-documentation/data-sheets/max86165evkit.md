## General Description

The MAX86165/MAX86168 evaluation kit (EV kit) provides a  quick  evaluation  of  the  MAX86165/MAX86168  optical AFE data-acquisition system.  The MAX86165/MAX86168 is  a  completely  integrated  optical  module.  MAX86165 transmitter side has a programmable low-noise LED driver with an IR emitter. MAX86168 transmitter side has a programmable low-noise LED driver with an IR emitter and a Green emitter. On the receiver side, MAX86165/MAX86168 consists of a high efficiency PIN photodiode and an optical readout channel. The MAX86165/MAX86168 AFE's readout channel provides a low noise signal conditioning AFE, including  a  20  bit ADC  and  an  industry  leading  ambient light  cancellation  (ALC)  circuit  which  results  in  a  94.5dB SNR in a white card loop-back test. Due to the low power consumption, compact size, ease and flexibility of use, the MAX86165/MAX86168 is ideal for a wide variety of optical sensing applications such as heart rate detection and prox -imity sensing in small spaces such as ear buds.

The  MAX86165/MAX86168  supports  I 2 C  compatible interfaces. The MAX86165/MAX86168 EV kit allows flex -ible configurations to optimize measurement signal quality at  minimal power consumption. The EV kit supports file and  flash  logging,  which  allows  the  user  to  disconnect from  the  computer  for  more  convenient  data  capturing sessions, such as overnight or outdoor running.

The MAX86165/MAX86168 EV kit consists of two boards. The  MAXSENSORBLE\_EVKIT\_B  is  the  data  acquisi -tion  board  while  the  MAX86165/MAX86168  OSBFLEX\_ EVKIT is the sensor daughter board for the MAX86165/ MAX86168.  The  EV  kit  is  powered  by  a  Li-Po  battery, which is  recharged  through  a  USB-C  cable. The  EV  kit streams data using Bluetooth® through Windows ®  BLE. The EV kit contains the latest  firmware  but  comes  with the programming circuit board MAXDAP-TYPE-C in case a firmware upgrade is needed.

Note:

This EV kit is not a medical device.
