## General Description

<!-- image -->

The  HMC326MS8G  &amp;  HMC326MS8GE  are  high efficiency GaAs InGaP Heterojunction Bipolar Transistor (HBT) MMIC driver amplifiers which operate  between  3.0  and  4.5  GHz.  The  amplifier is  packaged  in  a  low  cost,  surface  mount  8  leaded package with an exposed base for improved RF and thermal  performance.  The  amplifier  provides  21  dB of gain and +26 dBm of saturated power from a +5V supply voltage. Power down capability is available to conserve current consumption when the amplifier is not in use.  Internal circuit matching was optimized to provide greater than 40% PAE.
