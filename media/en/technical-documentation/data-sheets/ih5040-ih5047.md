## General-Purpose CMos Analog Switches

## General Description

The IH5040 family consists of seven CMOS analog tions.These switches arelatch-upproof,break-beforemake single,dual,and quad versions of the popular switch formats SPST,SPDT,DPST,and 4PST.Keyfeaturesofthefamilyincludealow,1nAleakagecurrentand a quiescent current of less than 1uA.

Maxim'sIH5040familyhasfasterswitchingtimesthan the original manufacturer's devices.All devices arebidirectionalandmaintainalmostconstantonresistance guaranteedtooperatefrom±4.5Vto±18V,andwillswitch input signals that include the supplies.
