## Typical Applications

Low noise QUADBAND MMIC VCO for:

- Test Equipment &amp; Industrial Controls
- VSAT Radio
- Point to Point/Multi-Point Radio
- Military End-Use

## General Description

The HMC8074LP6GE is a QUADBAND MMIC VCO that integrates the resonators, negative resistance devices, varactor diodes, and features two RF output pins. The VCO's phase noise performance is excellent over temperature, shock, and process due to the oscillator's monolithic structure. With a +4.75V supply, the output power is typically -5 to +6 dBm for each RF output, respectively. The voltage controlled oscillator is packaged in a leadless QFN 6x6 mm surface mount package, and requires no external matching components.
