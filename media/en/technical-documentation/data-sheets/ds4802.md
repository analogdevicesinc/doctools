## DESCRIPTION

The  DS4802  BiCMOS  dual  operational  amplifier  combines  low  input  offset  voltage,  very  low  power consumption, rail-to-rail output swing, and excellent DC precision.  With a maximum input offset voltage of 0.95 mV, a maximum IDD of 25 µ A/amplifier, and 10 pA typical input bias current, the DS4802 is ideal for measurement, medical, and industrial applications.  The DS4802 is also ideal for portable applications with 1.8 volt to 5.5 volt single supply voltage operation and low power consumption.
