## General Description

The HMC476SC70(E) is a SiGe Heterojunction Bipolar Transistor  (HBT)  Gain  Block  MMIC  SMT  amplifiers covering DC to 6 GHz. This industry standard SC70 packaged  amplifier  can  be  used  as  a  cascadable 50 Ohm RF/IF gain stage as well as a LO or PA driver with up to +12 dBm output power. The HMC476SC70(E) offers  20  dB  of  gain  with  a  +24  dBm  output  IP3  at 850  MHz  while  requiring  only  35  mA  from  a  single positive  supply.  The  Darlington  topology  results  in reduced sensitivity to normal process variations and excellent gain stability over temperature while requiring a minimal number of external bias components.
