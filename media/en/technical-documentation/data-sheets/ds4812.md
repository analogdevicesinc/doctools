## DESCRIPTION

The DS4812 BiCMOS dual operational amplifier combines high slew rate and rail-to-rail output swing. The device provides 10V/ µ s of slew rate and 6.5 MHz of bandwidth while only consuming 1.5 mA of supply current per channel.  Ideal low voltage BiFET substitute for low gain, high speed applications.
