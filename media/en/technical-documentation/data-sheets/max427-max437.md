The MAX427/MAX437 are very low-noise amplifiers. They achieve·outstandinginputvoltagenoisecharacteristics by operating the input stage at a highquiescent current. Input bias and offset currents, which would normally increase with the quiescent current, are minimized by bias-current cancellation circuitry. The MAX427/MAX437 haveIBandlosofonly±35nAand30nArespectivelyat +25°℃. This is particularly important with high sourceresistances.

Voltage noise is inversely proportional to the square-root of bias current, but current noise is proportional to the square-root of bias current. The MAX427/MAX437 lownoiseadvantagesarereducedwhenhighsourceresistors are used.
